# About Me

Hi! I'm Aljazei, a second-year student at NYU Abu Dhabi, majoring in **Interactive Media** with minors in **Film and New Media** and **Design**.

This website is part of my **Digital Humanities** class, where I've been exploring how technology can be used to study, analyze, and present information in different ways.

Throughout the semester, I've been working with digital tools, datasets, maps, and visualizations. I've also been learning how to use GitHub and Markdown to document my projects and share my work.
